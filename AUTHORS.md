## Roles

| Role | Who | Where it is recorded |
|---|---|---|
| Original author of this codebase | `Tesla_SHT` | `author` field in `package.json` |
| Current maintainer | [RxWhizz](https://github.com/RxWhizz) | `maintainers` field in `package.json` |
| Copyright holder | Corpus contributors | `LICENSE` |

The `author` field is preserved as originally recorded and is not reassigned
when maintainership changes. New work is attributed through commit history and
the `maintainers` field.

> **Maintainer action required before publishing packages.** The `author.email`
> in `package.json` is still the placeholder `corpus@localhost`, and the Linux
> package maintainer address is a GitHub no-reply placeholder. Neither blocks
> the v1.0 AppImage / NSIS / ZIP targets, but both should be set to real
> contact addresses before publishing `.deb` or `.rpm` packages or to npm.

## Derivative-work statement

Corpus implements workflows that are conventional in microscopy image analysis
— thresholding, watershed separation, shape filtering, object review, and
distribution summaries. These techniques are standard and are implemented here
independently, with Corpus's own TEM-focused naming, interface, data model and
documentation. Corpus is **not** a fork, port, or derivative of ImageJ/Fiji,
CVAT, Label Studio, or Ultralytics, and does not redistribute their code.

## Data attribution

- **`Examples/demo_dataset/`** — synthetic phantoms generated by
  `scripts/build_demo_dataset.py`, released under CC0-1.0. Freely
  redistributable. See its [README](Examples/demo_dataset/README.md).
- **Other files under `Examples/`** — working images kept for manual testing.
  Their provenance is not established, so they are excluded from the
  redistributable demo set and must not be republished as Corpus data.
- **Curated TEM sources** — every image ingested through the Corpus Builder
  carries `source_url`, `license` and `license_status` in `data/images.csv`.
  Images without an accepted licence are gated out of the public demo layer by
  `training/audit_training_dataset.py`.
